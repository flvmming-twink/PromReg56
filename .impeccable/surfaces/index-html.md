# Surface: industrial atlas

Mode: Operate. Public Russian-language web interface for finding enterprises and understanding how they connect to territories, industries and sources. The primary action is search; the proof is the actual database and visible provenance. Static GitHub Pages constraints apply.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A living industrial register built with the visual honesty of an equipment identification plate. It refuses the category default of interchangeable KPI cards, soft gradients and decorative analytics.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper-white and graphite fields, mineral blue for data, signal orange only for actions. Square geometry, clipped plate corners, thin ruled rows, large identifiers and compact label/value pairs. Raised by the split-flap board's disciplined rows, the catalog's linked index, the consumer surface's action-only accent and terminal signage's one-decision-at-a-time clarity.

STORY: The visitor first sees what is actually in the database, then searches or selects a territory, opens an enterprise passport and checks its products, metrics and sources. Missing information is stated plainly. No synthetic trends or inactive controls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slim masthead and section rail sit above a full-width working field. The left two-thirds hold a coordinate map of all enterprises at useful scale; the right third is a numbered regional index. A factual count strip and a prominent search line bridge both. The primary action sits inside the search line, not in a promotional hero.

FORM: Grounded candidate 5, industrial certification plate, seed `07421053`. The signature interaction is the linked passport: selecting a map point or registry row opens the same detail sheet, while the counterpart on the map or table is located and highlighted. Motion is one short mechanical slide; reduced-motion users get an immediate state change.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
